How we use your data

We use your name and email to handle your order and to write to you if needed. If you choose shipping, we use your address and phone number only to send you the parcel: only the courier sees them.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, and we don't use it for newsletters or advertising. We only send the makers the number of items per size, never names.

We delete emails, addresses and phone numbers once all the deliveries of the preorder are done. To see, correct or delete them sooner, write to us at thecirconvalley@gmail.com.
