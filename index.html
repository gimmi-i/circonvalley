Circonvalley Bikes

Circonvalley is a cycling community.

Our goal is to help you grow your passion for bicycles by connecting you with other people who love cycling. We organize social events and rides, we share ideas for new adventures, and we help repair and build bikes. We are from Milano, we know that cycling in a big city isn't always easy, and that's why we are here: to help everyone find their own way into cycling.

Whether you are a commuter, a bikepacker, a messenger or a bike racer, you are welcome to join our community and be part of what we do.
